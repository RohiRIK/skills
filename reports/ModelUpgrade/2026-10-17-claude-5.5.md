## Summary

1. **Art's workflows still pointed at skills and paths from the donor library.** Sixteen workflow lines called `~/.claude/skills/art/Tools/Generate.ts`, but the folder is `Art`, so the path fails on a case-sensitive filesystem. Background removal went through a `CORE/Tools/RemoveBg.ts` tool and an "Images skill", neither of which exists; the skill's own `RemoveBackground` workflow (local `rembg`) now does that job. `Essay.md` still referred to a "Create Story Explanation run", called `nano-banana-pro` the default model when `Generation.md` makes `agy` the default, and one of its examples wrote output straight into a project directory, which breaks the skill's own Downloads-first gate. All of these were model-neutral stale facts and are fixed. The de-prescription replan the last run proposed (24 pressure-language hits and 850 lines of step choreography in `Essay.md`) still needs an A/B test on real image output, so Art stays unstamped.
2. **SecurityReview depends on an agent the repo doesn't ship.** It forks `security-reviewer` "and does not run inline", with no fallback, which breaks the binding host-portability rule (`rules/system.md` §7). Its `AuditCode` workflow is two sentences long and never loads the `Overview.md` checklist that `SKILL.md` says it loads. Both of those facts are fixed. The skill is still a `replan` because it is a chain step in three workflows (`api-feature`, `security-pass`, `build-mcp`), it shares its job and nearly its name with Claude Code's built-in `security-review`, and its report format gives `security-pass` no severity or confidence to filter on.
3. **Reflect is a keep, and it is now stamped.** Its text is clean (no scan hits). Its job is structural: `Iterate`'s exit check reads Reflect's score. The ledger's warning about verification scaffolding applies to "re-check your answer" lines inside other prompts, and says to re-test before deleting them on Opus 5.5, not that they are harmful there. Fable 5.1, in the same generation, benefits from test-before-reporting. One open question stays at low confidence; see the table.

Either ship a `security-reviewer` agent definition or drop `context: fork` and `agent:` so the skill runs inline as Tier C. Renaming the skill is the owner's call.
