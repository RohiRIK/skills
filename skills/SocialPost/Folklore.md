# Folklore: claims that are not rules

Advice that circulates in creator courses and other skills without a platform source. Read this file only to answer "should I…?" questions. Nothing here becomes a lint rule or a hard instruction. Checked 2026-10-12.

| Claim | Where it comes from | What to say instead |
|---|---|---|
| Put the link in the first comment, not the post | Creator lore from about 2019; LinkedIn denies a link penalty, and 2026 creator data found the comment trick no longer better | Tell the user the trade-off; the post must work without the click |
| The first 60 to 90 minutes, the "golden hour", decide reach | Creator lore; early engagement correlates with reach, but no platform defines a window | Post when the audience is awake; reply to early comments because people are there, not to beat a timer |
| Facebook posts of 40 to 80 characters get 66% more engagement | A 2012 blog post, still copied into 2026 skills | Length follows the content; the hook goes inside the fold |
| A save is worth 5 likes; a comment is worth 5 to 15 | Creator courses; no methodology published | X's open code publishes its own weights (`ShortForm.md`); other platforms don't |
| "360Brew" is LinkedIn's live ranking model | A LinkedIn research paper, not a statement about production | LinkedIn's production ranker is described in its engineering blog; say only that it reads post text |
| LinkedIn cuts reach of AI-detected posts by 30%, engagement by 55% | Viral posts with no source | LinkedIn demotes generic slop and bait, by its own statement; write specifically |
| Editing a post in the first 10 minutes kills its reach | Creator lore | Fix typos when you see them |
| Hashtags multiply reach | Pre-2024 advice | LinkedIn removed hashtag following; Instagram caps tags at 5 and says they categorize, not spread |
| X recommends 2 hashtags | Old X marketing advice | 0 to 1 on X |
| Use 10 to 30 Hebrew hashtags on Instagram | Pre-2025 Israeli guides | At most 5 since December 2025 |
| Hook formulas guarantee reach multipliers | Hook-library sellers | A hook earns the click; the content earns the reach |
| Make it undetectable by AI detectors | Humanizer tools | Detectors misfire, especially on non-native English. Aim for a post that reads like this person, not for a detector score |
