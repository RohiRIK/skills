# Channels and video: WhatsApp, Telegram, TikTok, YouTube

Limits: `Platforms.md`. Checked 2026-10-12. WhatsApp and Telegram channels are major news and community channels in Israel; treat them as first-class.

## WhatsApp Channels

- One-way broadcast to followers, with polls and voice updates. Polls: a question up to 255 characters, up to 12 options of 100 characters (reported).
- **Native formatting is allowed and expected here**: `*bold*`, `_italic_`, `~strike~`, ```` ```monospace``` ````, `- ` and `1. ` lists, `> ` quotes. Never markdown's `**double**`.
- Shape: a short first line that works as a headline, 2 to 4 short paragraphs, one link at most.
- Israeli law restricts unsolicited commercial messages ("חוק הספאם"). A channel people chose to follow is fine; adding people to commercial broadcast groups without consent is not (`Disclosure.md`).

## Telegram channels

- Messages up to 4,096 characters, media captions up to 1,024 (`Platforms.md`). An image with more text than that: send the image with a short caption, then the text as its own message.
- Rich formatting and clickable text links work.
- Shape: a bold headline line, 2 to 4 short paragraphs, one link.
- Night or low-priority updates: suggest Telegram's silent send.

## TikTok captions

- TikTok works like a search engine. Put the phrase people search for at the very start of the caption: "איך לנקות מזגן לבד", "how to clean an AC filter". Creator Search Insights shows what people search.
- Tell the user to say the same phrase aloud early in the video and show it as on-screen text; the caption alone carries less.
- Caption: the keyword line, one line of context, 3 to 5 specific hashtags. Links aren't clickable.

## YouTube

- **Title**: up to 100 characters, but about 60 show in results. The keyword and the promise first.
- **Description**: the first 2 to 3 lines show above the fold, so they summarize the video and carry the keyword. Links after that.
- **Hashtags**: the first 3 in the description show above the title. More than 60 and YouTube ignores all of them.
- **Two languages**: write separate native titles and descriptions per language with YouTube's translations feature, rather than relying on auto-translation.
- **Posts** (formerly the Community tab): treat like a short Facebook post.
