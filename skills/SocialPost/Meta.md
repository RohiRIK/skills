# Meta: Facebook and Instagram

Limits and folds: `Platforms.md`. Checked 2026-10-12. Facebook is the largest public social network for Israeli adults and Facebook Groups carry local commerce and community, so Hebrew Facebook gets first-class treatment here.

## What Meta penalizes

- **Engagement bait**, demoted since 2017 in five kinds: vote bait ("react 😮 for A, ❤️ for B"), react bait ("like if…"), share bait ("share with 10 friends"), tag bait ("tag a friend who…"), and comment bait ("comment YES if…"). Pages that do it repeatedly are demoted as a whole. Genuine requests for help, advice, or recommendations are exempt.
- **Spammy captions** (April 2025): long captions stuffed with unrelated hashtags, or captions that don't match the content.
- **Unoriginal content** (July 2025): reposting other people's text, photos, or video without meaningful change. Pasting the same text into many Groups or Pages is risky.
- **Links on Facebook**: Meta tested limiting non-subscribed professional profiles and Pages to 2 link posts a month from December 2025; links in comments were not limited. Check the current status with the user before planning around links.

## Facebook posts

- **Profile**: personal, longer, story-shaped. The hook within about 125 characters for mobile; then as long as the story needs.
- **Page**: the business voice, plural "we" (אנחנו). Offers carry the terms, the deadline, and a disclosure where required (`Disclosure.md`).
- **Short text on a colored background** is limited to about 130 characters; use it for one sentence, not a message.
- **Hashtags**: 0 to 3, at the end.
- **No naming and shaming** of a business or a person in a complaint post: describe the problem, not the culprit. Defamation claims over Facebook posts reach Israeli courts.

## Facebook Groups

Each group's pinned rules decide what's allowed, and admins enforce them, often with tools that auto-decline posts containing links or posts from new members.
1. Get the rules first: ask the user to paste them, or ask about self-promotion and links.
2. **Value first**: the problem the members have, then the tip or the story, then, only if the rules allow, a soft mention of the business with disclosure.
3. **Recommendation requests** (very common in Israeli groups): what you need, where, by when, the budget if relevant, one line on what matters most. Thank the group in advance once.
4. **Posting to several groups**: vary the opening and the order; never paste identical text.
5. Never ask members to comment a word, tag friends, or share for a reward unless it's a giveaway with published rules (`Disclosure.md`).

## Instagram

- **Captions are search text**: Instagram search reads them, and since July 2025 search engines can index public posts from professional accounts. Put the words people search for in the first line.
- **Fold**: about 125 characters in the feed, about 55 on Reels. The hook and the keyword go first.
- **Hashtags**: at most 5 per post or Reel since December 2025. Mosseri says hashtags categorize a post rather than spread it. Specific beats generic: `#reels` and `#explore` do nothing.
- **Links aren't clickable** in captions for normal accounts. Write where the link is: "the link is in the bio" (הלינק בביו), or a story link. Not "comment LINK", which is comment bait.
- **What Instagram ranks** (Mosseri, January 2025): watch time, likes per reach, and sends per reach. Content people send to a friend reaches non-followers.
- **Structure**: hook, 1 to 3 short paragraphs, one specific call to action, tags last. A space before a line break deletes the break.
- **Alt text**: write it for every image, short and literal.
