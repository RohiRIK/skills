# Templates: WhatsApp, Telegram, TikTok, YouTube

Formatting and limits: `Channels.md`. WhatsApp and Telegram have their own bold markers; markdown still doesn't belong anywhere else.

## WhatsApp Channel update

**Moves:** a headline in WhatsApp bold · what's new, in two or three short sentences · what followers should do · one link at most.

```text whatsapp en
*[Headline: the news in a few words]*

[What happened or what's new, in two or three short sentences.]

[What followers should do, if anything.]

[One link, if needed]
```

```text whatsapp he
*[כותרת: החדשה בכמה מילים]*

[מה קרה או מה חדש, בשניים-שלושה משפטים קצרים.]

[מה כדאי לעוקבים לעשות, אם בכלל.]

[קישור אחד, אם צריך]
```

## Telegram channel post

**Moves:** a bold headline · the update in two to four short paragraphs · the source or link. With an image and more than 1,024 characters, send the image with a short caption, then the text as its own message.

```text telegram en
**[Headline]**

[The update, in two to four short paragraphs.]

[Source or link]
```

```text telegram he
**[כותרת]**

[העדכון, בשתיים עד ארבע פסקאות קצרות.]

[מקור או קישור]
```

## TikTok caption

**Moves:** the search phrase first · the promise in a few words · one line of context · 3 to 5 specific tags. Remind the user to say the phrase early in the video and show it on screen.

```text tiktok en
[The search phrase first, such as "how to clean an AC filter"]: [the promise, in a few words].

[One line of context.]

#[topic] #[topic] #[topic]
```

```text tiktok he
[ביטוי החיפוש קודם, למשל: "איך לנקות פילטר של מזגן"]: [ההבטחה, בכמה מילים].

[שורת הקשר אחת.]

#[נושא] #[נושא] #[נושא]
```

## YouTube title and description

**Moves:** title: the keyword and the promise inside about 60 characters · description: two summary lines with the search phrase, then timestamps, links, and up to 3 tags. For two languages, add a native title and description per language with YouTube's translations feature.

```text youtube-title en
[Keyword first]: [the promise] in [number] minutes
```

```text youtube-title he
[מילת מפתח]: [ההבטחה] ב-[מספר] דקות
```

```text youtube-desc en
[Two lines that summarize the video and include the search phrase.]

What's in the video:
0:00 [Intro]
1:15 [Step one]
4:30 [Step two]

[Links mentioned in the video]

#[topic] #[topic] #[topic]
```

```text youtube-desc he
[שתי שורות שמסכמות את הסרטון וכוללות את ביטוי החיפוש.]

מה יש בסרטון:
0:00 [פתיחה]
1:15 [שלב ראשון]
4:30 [שלב שני]

[קישורים שהוזכרו בסרטון]

#[נושא] #[נושא] #[נושא]
```
