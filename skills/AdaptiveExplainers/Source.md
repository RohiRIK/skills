# Source and interpretation

Source: https://x.com/karpathy/status/2105819303471976479?s=46

Karpathy covers four methods: controlled-language writing (ASD-STE100 or softened approximation), diagrams/images, HTML web pages, and bespoke explainer videos. He uses “even better” transitions and especially highlights video. He argues that autonomous work increases the importance of oversight and understanding and makes custom discardable software practical.

He provides no numeric complexity scale and does not require every hard question to become a video. Matching format to understanding burden is our adaptation. Small interactive tools fit his custom-software argument; they are not a fifth explicitly named rung.

The originating owner initially requested HTML flowcharts for complex output, then corrected the skill's scope to include the full set of methods according to complexity/usefulness. HTML remains preferred for complex workflows, not the sole delivery method. English, mobile-friendly delivery, evidence, verification, and safety gates are our implementation choices.
